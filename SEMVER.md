# SEMVER — centerfugeq versioning

The floors repo follows Semantic Versioning from `0.1.0`. Under 0.x:

- **PATCH**: a floor fix, a harness fix, docs.
- **MINOR**: a new floor or modality (a playable artifact IS a feature).
- The **smoke harness** (`scripts/check-floors.mjs`) is the release gate:
  a version that breaks a floor is a documented MINOR at minimum.
